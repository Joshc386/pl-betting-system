Backtest S25, 98 bets — hit rate rises monotonically with agreement:

| `n_agree` | Bets | Hit rate |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2 | 50.0% |
| 3 | 18 | 55.6% |
| 4 | 78 | **61.5%** |

Live settled recommendations say the **opposite**:

| Agreement | Bets | Hit rate | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4/4 | 29 | 34.5% | −4.1% |
| 3/4 | 6 | 16.7% | −56.4% |
| DC only (alt lines) | 8 | **75.0%** | **+12.2%** |

(4/4 claims +8.7% EV and returns −4.1%; DC-only claims +1.9% and returns +12.2%). The inversion
you would expect from upward-miscalibrated ensemble confidence.

## Gotchas discovered this session

**17 of the 60 settled PL recommendations have fake unanimity.** All four model probabilities
byte-identical to 16 decimal places — one blended number written into every slot, then counted
as four agreements. Confined to **8–10 April 2026**; every row from 13 April on is clean. They
inflated the 4/4 bucket by 24pp. `_model_agreement` returns `"!"` for them and the breakdown
excludes them with a visible footnote. The exclusion rule is structural (≥2 models, all values
identical), not a date filter.

**`assert_known_teams` guards only the dataset-build path, never the live path.** It is called
in `data/build_canonical_dataset.py:245` and `data/add_season.py:40` — not in scan or predict.
An unmapped club therefore breaks the *ingest*, silently, on data that does not exist yet, while
every live path keeps working. A test exercising the live paths would have passed.

**`per_bet_roi` is stake-invariant.** A win contributes `odds-1`, a loss `-1`, regardless of
stake. So `edge_analytics.bootstrap_ci` (mean of ratios) can never bracket a stake-weighted
point estimate (ratio of sums), and the Kelly and flat rows of the removed counterfactual table
were mathematically guaranteed to show identical CIs around different numbers. Verified on real
data: `max abs diff 4.44e-16`. New `_bootstrap_roi_ci` in `dashboard.py` resamples bet indices
and recomputes the ratio of sums.

**Live ROI will not be a usable validation signal for years.** SD of per-bet ROI is 1.17, so
±5pp needs ~2,100 bets and ±2pp needs ~13,100. At ~10 bets/week that is four years for the
former. CLV and calibration have to carry validation.

**Never `grep -r` from the project root.** OneDrive makes it time out, and it walks
`.claude/worktrees/` first. Use the Grep/Glob tools.
