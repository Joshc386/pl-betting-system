## The finding, in its final form

Agreement's dependable signal is **negative and one-directional**. Three bins have 95%
intervals excluding zero and **every one is unanimous opposition**: PL O/U 2.5 −5.85%,
PL BTTS −6.18%, EFL O/U 1.5 −2.54%. **No unanimous-support bin clears zero.**

The ensemble is dependable about what **not** to back, and not yet demonstrably
dependable about what to back.

**This is a strategy finding, not a strategy change.** `min_agree=2` was not touched and
must not be without an explicit ask. Note the value that does exist sits on the opposite
side from where the gate spends its selectivity.

**Two traps for anyone redoing this** (both pinned by tests):
1. **Never pool cells.** PL's three markets pooled give +2.15% — two real signals diluted
   by one null.
2. **The pre-gate view is antisymmetric.** Both sides force
   `n_agree(a) + n_agree(b) = n_models`, so bins 0 and N are mirrors with exactly
   opposite realised edges, and a 4-model cell's middle bin returns precisely `+0.00%`.
   The shipped view keeps `side_a` only.
